Los Santos & Blaine County

The game is set across the fictional state of San Andreas — a sun-bleached parody of Southern California, anchored by the sprawling city of Los Santos and stretching out into the desert, mountains, and small towns of Blaine County.

It was, at launch, the most detailed and widest-ranging open world Rockstar had built, and the map has stayed in near-constant use ever since as the backdrop for GTA Online.

Three leads, one heist

The first mainline game to let you switch between three protagonists mid-mission.

Michael

A retired bank robber living an uneasy, unhappy version of the good life under a new identity — pulled back in when his past catches up with him.

Franklin

A young repo man looking for a way out of low-level hustling, who sees a shot at something bigger once his path crosses Michael's.

Trevor

Michael's volatile former partner, running his own operation out in Blaine County — the loose thread that unravels everyone's cover.
